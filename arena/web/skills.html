Skill registry

A skill executed inside the trusted runtime is code running with the operator's authority. Star count is not evidence of fitness for that. Skills are discovered automatically, promoted by a recorded human decision, and measured before promotion — not after.

Three registries, three lifecycles

What we have found, what we have approved, and what we measure against are different objects. Conflating them is how a leaderboard silently becomes meaningless.

The promotion path is one-way

A monthly update may never modify the Benchmark Registry. Otherwise the current month's score and last month's are not on the same scale.

Entries

Stable skills are the only ones the runtime resolves. Candidate skills are listed with their requested permissions because that is the thing a reviewer has to judge — a candidate that asks for network:any or exec:python is asking for more than its benchmark delta can justify.

Held candidates have been measured and not promoted.

A benchmark delta is the measured change in the named metric when the skill is added to the reference stack, over the stated number of cases. not measured means exactly that — a candidate is never promoted on the strength of an unmeasured claim. Permission chips marked ⚠ are the ones that widen the runtime's authority.

How a skill is promoted

  1. Discovery. The monthly pipeline scans upstream projects and newly published SKILL.md files. It may write only to the Candidate Catalog. Candidate skills are never executed in production.
  2. Licence and security review. Run once, at promotion, rather than on every execution. The review records the licence determination and the permission set.
  3. Measurement. The candidate is benchmarked against the reference stack on the current Season, producing a delta with a case count. A negative or absent delta holds the promotion.
  4. Decision. A PromotionDecision is recorded by a named reviewer. It is the only writer of a Stable Registry revision; there is no automatic path, and no popularity threshold.
  5. Season cut. A benchmark Season is cut from a Stable revision and is thereafter immutable. Promote into the registry, and the next Season picks it up; never into the Season.

TCMScience Arena. A read-only evaluation surface. It renders published result bundles; it never computes a score, never re-ranks, and never writes a registry.

Static HTML, one stylesheet, one script. No server, no database, no cookies, no tracking.
