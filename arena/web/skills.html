Skill registry技能注册表

A skill executed inside the trusted runtime is code running with the operator's authority. Star count is not evidence of fitness for that. Skills are discovered automatically, promoted by a recorded human decision, and measured before promotion — not after. 在可信运行时内执行的技能，是带着运营方权限运行的代码。星标数不是它适合这一角色的证据。技能被自动发现、由一次被记录的人类决策晋级，并且在晋级之前就被测量——不是之后。

Three registries, three lifecycles三个注册表，三种生命周期

What we have found, what we have approved, and what we measure against are different objects. Conflating them is how a leaderboard silently becomes meaningless. 「我们发现了什么」「我们批准了什么」「我们拿什么做测量基准」是三个不同的对象。把它们混为一谈，就是一个排行榜悄悄变得毫无意义的开始。

The promotion path is one-way晋级路径是单向的

A monthly update may never modify the Benchmark Registry. Otherwise the current month's score and last month's are not on the same scale. 月度更新永远不能改动基准注册表（Benchmark Registry）。否则本月与上月的分数就不在同一把尺子上。

Entries条目

Stable skills are the only ones the runtime resolves. Candidate skills are listed with their requested permissions because that is the thing a reviewer has to judge — a candidate that asks for network:any or exec:python is asking for more than its benchmark delta can justify. 运行时只解析稳定技能。候选技能连同它们申请的权限一并列出，因为那正是审阅者必须判断的东西——一个申请 network:any 或 exec:python 的候选，所索取的已经超出它的基准增量所能辩护的范围。

Held candidates have been measured and not promoted.被暂缓的候选已经过测量，但尚未晋级。

A benchmark delta is the measured change in the named metric when the skill is added to the reference stack, over the stated number of cases. not measured means exactly that — a candidate is never promoted on the strength of an unmeasured claim. Permission chips marked ⚠ are the ones that widen the runtime's authority.

基准增量是指：把该技能加入参考技术栈后，在所述题目数量上，指定指标的实测变化。not measured 就是这个意思——候选永远不会因为一条未经测量的说法而晋级。标有 ⚠ 的权限标签，是那些会扩大运行时权限的权限。

How a skill is promoted一个技能如何晋级

  1. Discovery. The monthly pipeline scans upstream projects and newly published SKILL.md files. It may write only to the Candidate Catalog. Candidate skills are never executed in production. 发现。月度流水线扫描上游项目和新发布的 SKILL.md 文件。它只能写入候选目录（Candidate Catalog）。候选技能永远不在生产环境中执行。
  2. Licence and security review. Run once, at promotion, rather than on every execution. The review records the licence determination and the permission set. 许可与安全审阅。只在晋级时运行一次，而不是每次执行都跑。这次审阅记录下许可判定与权限集。
  3. Measurement. The candidate is benchmarked against the reference stack on the current Season, producing a delta with a case count. A negative or absent delta holds the promotion. 测量。候选会在当前赛季上对着参考技术栈做基准测试，产出一个带题目数量的增量。增量为负或缺失，就暂缓晋级。
  4. Decision. A PromotionDecision is recorded by a named reviewer. It is the only writer of a Stable Registry revision; there is no automatic path, and no popularity threshold. 决策。一条 PromotionDecision 由具名审阅者记录。它是稳定注册表（Stable Registry）修订版的唯一写入者；没有自动路径，也没有热度门槛。
  5. Season cut. A benchmark Season is cut from a Stable revision and is thereafter immutable. Promote into the registry, and the next Season picks it up; never into the Season. 赛季切出。一个基准赛季从稳定修订版中切出，此后不可更改。要晋级就晋到注册表里，下一个赛季会把它带上；永远不要晋到赛季里。

TCMScience Arena. A read-only evaluation surface. It renders published result bundles; it never computes a score, never re-ranks, and never writes a registry. 只读评测平台。它渲染已发布的结果包；从不计算分数、从不重新排名、也从不向任何注册表写入。

Static HTML, one stylesheet, one script. No server, no database, no cookies, no tracking.纯静态 HTML，一个样式表、一个脚本。无服务器、无数据库、无 Cookie、无追踪。
