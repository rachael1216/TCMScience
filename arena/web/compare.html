Compare two systems对比两个系统

Track by track and dimension by dimension. Two systems can share an aggregate and disagree on every dimension underneath it, which is the reason the aggregate is never shown alone. 逐条赛道、逐项维度。两个系统可以综合分相同，却在它下面的每一项维度上都不一致——这就是综合分绝不单独显示的原因。

Shareable as compare.html?a=<system>&b=<system>可分享为 compare.html?a=<system>&b=<system>

TCMScience Arena. A read-only evaluation surface. It renders published result bundles; it never computes a score, never re-ranks, and never writes a registry. 只读评测平台。它渲染已发布的结果包；从不计算分数、从不重新排名、也从不向任何注册表写入。

Static HTML, one stylesheet, one script. No server, no database, no cookies, no tracking.纯静态 HTML，一个样式表、一个脚本。无服务器、无数据库、无 Cookie、无追踪。
