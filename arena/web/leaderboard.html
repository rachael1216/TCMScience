Results

Scores are reproduced exactly as the result generator published them. This page sorts and filters; it does not compute, re-rank, or round. Bars are a reading aid — the number is the information.

Column definitions, the aggregation formula and the gate statements are on the各列定义、聚合公式与门槛说明见 methods page方法页. Raw latencies and costs are shown under their subscores; the subscores exist so those two axes can enter a geometric mean.

TCMScience Arena. A read-only evaluation surface. It renders published result bundles; it never computes a score, never re-ranks, and never writes a registry.

Static HTML, one stylesheet, one script. No server, no database, no cookies, no tracking.
