One row per system per track. The eight dimensions are always visible; the aggregate is published alongside them and never instead of them. Click a row for the run. 每个系统、每条赛道一行。八项维度永远可见；综合分与它们一并发布，而绝不代替它们。点击一行即可查看该次运行。

Results结果

Scores are reproduced exactly as the result generator published them. This page sorts and filters; it does not compute, re-rank, or round. Bars are a reading aid — the number is the information. 分数按结果生成器发布的原样复现。本页只做排序与筛选；它不计算、不重新排名、也不做四舍五入。条形只是阅读辅助——数字本身才是信息。

Column definitions, the aggregation formula and the gate statements are on the methods page. Raw latencies and costs are shown under their subscores; the subscores exist so those two axes can enter a geometric mean.

各列定义、聚合公式与门槛说明见方法页。原始延迟与成本显示在各自子分数之下；子分数的存在，是为了让这两条轴能进入几何平均。

TCMScience Arena. A read-only evaluation surface. It renders published result bundles; it never computes a score, never re-ranks, and never writes a registry. 只读评测平台。它渲染已发布的结果包；从不计算分数、从不重新排名、也从不向任何注册表写入。

Static HTML, one stylesheet, one script. No server, no database, no cookies, no tracking.纯静态 HTML，一个样式表、一个脚本。无服务器、无数据库、无 Cookie、无追踪。
